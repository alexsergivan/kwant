# Commercial licensing

kwant is open source under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) (AGPL-3.0). Anyone can use it for free, for any purpose, as long as they follow the AGPL. The main condition: if you change kwant and let others use it over a network, you must offer them your changed source code under the AGPL too.

A commercial license removes those conditions. You probably want one if:

- **You sell it.** You sell or license kwant, a fork or a derivative work, or include it in a paid product, and don't want to publish your source code.
- **You host it for others.** You offer kwant as a hosted, managed or embedded service and want to keep your changes private.
- **Your company can't use AGPL software.** Your legal team doesn't allow AGPL code, and you need different terms.

You don't need one if you use kwant unchanged, or if you're happy to share your changes under the AGPL.

Versions published before the switch, including mac-v0.1.0, stay under MIT OR Apache-2.0.

## Getting a license

Write to contact@kwant.si with your company name, roughly how you plan to use kwant, and how many people will use it. We'll reply with terms.

If you're not sure whether you need a license, ask. We'd rather help you use kwant than turn you away.
