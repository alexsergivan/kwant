# Commercial licensing

kwant is free for most people under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE). You need a commercial license only if one of these applies:

- **You sell it.** You sell or license kwant, a fork or a derivative work, or include it in a paid product.
- **You host it for others.** You offer kwant as a hosted, managed or embedded service.
- **Your company is large.** Your company and its affiliates had €1M or more in gross revenue in the most recent fiscal year, and you use kwant in production.

You don't need one for personal use, non-profit, education or research, or for a company under €1M using kwant for its own work, including work it delivers to clients. Testing and evaluating kwant is always free.

Each version becomes Apache-2.0 four years after it's published. Versions published before the switch, including mac-v0.1.0, stay under MIT OR Apache-2.0.

## Getting a license

Write to contact@kwant.si with your company name, roughly how you plan to use kwant, and how many people will use it. We'll reply with terms.

If you're not sure whether you need a license, ask. We'd rather help you use kwant than turn you away.
