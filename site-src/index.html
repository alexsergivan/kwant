Open-source, self-hosted AI agents in one binary

Your own self-hosted team of AI agents

They share chats, pass work to each other and get it done on their own, on a schedule or while you're away. It runs on your laptop or a VPS.

Spaces & accounts

Keep work, side projects and home apart

Each space has its own bots, chats, routines and accounts. Nothing leaks between them.

A space for each part of your life

Give it a name, an icon and a colour. Switch with ⌘/Ctrl + 1–9, and each space opens where you left it.

Your team

A real team, not another chat tab

While you're away

It keeps going when you close the tab

Routines run on a schedule, bots check back on their own, and kwant keeps track of what's still open.

Say "every morning", get a routine

The bot sets it up itself, with Undo, Edit and Pause on the card. Or make one: daily, weekly, every few minutes, or a cron rule.

Up and running in three steps

You need Rust and Node.js to build it, and one AI account.

  1. 1

    Build and run

    make run

    The web app opens on your machine. On a server, open the link it prints once.

  2. 2

    Add an account

    Claude CodeCodexGrokAPI key

    Use a CLI you're already signed in to, or paste a key. Add as many as you like.

  3. 3

    Ask your team

    "Every weekday at 9, check the deploys."

    The bot answers, or sets up a routine with an Undo button.
