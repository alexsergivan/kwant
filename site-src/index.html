First time on a Mac?macOS asks once, because kwant isn't notarized by Apple yet.
  1. Drag kwant to Applications and open it. When macOS says it couldn't verify it, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to “kwant” was blocked, then Open Anyway again, and enter your Mac password or use Touch ID.

You only do this once. On macOS 14 or earlier, right-click kwant in Applications and choose Open instead.

Yes, go ahead

When the bot's newest message asks you something, 👍 means Yes, go ahead and 👎 means No; hover a thumb to see which. Pick one and the bot carries on without you typing. Anywhere else, a 👍 or 👎 is feedback the bot reads next time.

Up and running in three steps

You need Rust and Node.js to build it, and one AI account.

  1. 1

    Build and run

    make run

    The web app opens on your machine. On a server, open the link it prints once.

  2. 2

    Add an account

    Claude CodeCodexGrokAPI key

    Use a CLI you're already signed in to, or paste a key. Add as many as you like.

  3. 3

    Ask your team

    "Every weekday at 9, check the deploys."

    The bot answers, or sets up a routine with an Undo button.
