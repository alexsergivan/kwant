New in 0.9.7

Your bot at work while a reply is coming, and which AI each bot runs on

While a reply is coming, you see the bot itself at work instead of grey lines. Each bot's avatar shows the AI it runs on, live while it works. One search box finds a tool, built in, through Composio or from the public directory.

  • Your bot at work while a reply is coming

    Before the first words of a reply arrive, the bubble shows the bot's own avatar at a small pixel laptop: it types, the screen fills and clears, and the line next to it says Ben is working on it. In a team chat it's the bot that is speaking. The first words fade in where the picture was, at the same height, so nothing jumps. With reduced motion on, the picture stands still, and screen readers hear "Ben is working on the answer".

  • Each bot's AI on its avatar

    A small mark at the bottom left of a bot's avatar shows the AI it runs on: Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Ollama, a chip for a custom engine, or ✦ when Laya picks the model. While a bot works, the mark shows what it runs on right now and changes if the bot moves to another engine. A resting bot that Laya picks for shows its last reply's mark, faded, with a small ✦. A pinned bot whose engine is signed out, off or not set up is greyed, and the tooltip says why: Runs on Claude · Sonnet · signed out. Running out of usage doesn't grey it.

  • Find a tool in one search box

    One search box finds a tool to add, in Add a tool and on Apps & accounts. Suggestions start from the first letter and forgive typos and short names ("gmial", "11labs", "pg"). Results come in three groups: Built in, Through Composio, and From the public directory, a copy of the official MCP Registry that kwant refreshes every 6 hours. Add & test installs a tool from there in one step. Connecting a Composio app before Composio is set up asks for the key right there.

  • Smaller changes in 0.9.7

    Through Composio finds any Composio app. Signing in to tool servers like ElevenLabs works.

New in 0.9.6

A team from your own words, and what your bots did while you were away

Describe what you need and kwant suggests a team. The Inbox shows what your bots finished while you were away, with the answer, what they did and why. A bot that stops either continues by itself or asks you once. Settings says in one card whether you're on the latest kwant. kwant's Chrome looks like normal Chrome, and opens a window when Google insists on one.

A team from your words

Write what your bots should do in the "What should your bots do?" box on the Get ready card, from Describe your own in the sidebar, or with "Describe what this team should do" in New team, and press Suggest a team. One of your ready AI accounts drafts it, without tools, usually in under a minute. The Suggested team sheet shows 2 to 6 bots with the lead first: untick or rename them, or Try again with the same words, then Create team, with Undo. The draft never gives a bot tools, accounts or routines, and the team's pinned instructions quote your words.

Done while you were away

The Inbox has two groups: Needs you and Done while you were away. Done items stay for 7 days, seen or not, and the badge counts only the ones you haven't read. Open one and you see what ran, when and why, the answer with Open in chat, a What Ben did trail with times, and Why: what was asked, or the routine's instruction with Edit routine. Chips say "Read only · changed nothing" or "No checks set · Add one". Answer with Looks good or Ask Ben to redo…; Roll back is hidden when nothing changed. In a routine's History, each run has Open result ›.

A stopped bot carries on or asks you

When a reply in your own chat stops before it's done, it either continues by itself, a limited number of times, or leaves you exactly one ask: one line in the chat with Retry or Continue, and one item in Needs you that says what stopped and why. The item closes when you write in that chat or when the bot's next reply there finishes. When kwant restarts in the middle of a reply in your own chat, that reply continues by itself once; if it can't, you get "Ben's reply was cut off when kwant restarted. Retry in the chat sends your message again." A failed hand-off or routine run keeps its one Inbox entry, and a mission task kwant stops gets one line in its chat.

Apps, AI accounts and tool servers now share one settings page, and connecting an app takes 3 steps. Each bot can have a monthly budget. When a bot's connection stops working, its chat says which one and why, and the team browser copes when it's busy.

One line when a connection stops working

When a bot's tool fails because of its connection, the chat gets one line that names it and says why, for example "GitHub stopped working for Ben: it needs you to sign in again. Reconnect". New in 0.7.7

Keys safe from tricked Claude bots, and kwant on Windows

On Linux, a web page, file or bot that tricks Claude can't make it read your sign-ins or keys: Claude works in a sealed box with no internet. kwant now has a Windows installer (beta). Get started can bring your bots from other apps, and Dev mode reviews only fresh work.

Protect your keys from tricked Claude bots

If a web page, a file or another bot talks Claude into misbehaving, it still can't read your sign-ins or keys, or go online by itself: anything it does outside goes through kwant's own tools, under the bot's approval settings. In VM mode, the switch Protect your keys from tricked Claude bots (called Run Claude inside the VM in 0.7.7) runs each bot's Claude replies in a sealed box with no internet that sees only the task folder. Your real sign-in stays outside: kwant passes on only the model calls Claude needs, up to 200 per reply, and refuses everything else.
