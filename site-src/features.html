Teams & chat

A team of bots that talk to each other

Each bot has a role, a voice and a model, and knows who else is in the room.

Teams with a lead

Give a team a mission, pinned instructions every member follows, a lead and a shared workspace.

{{shot:desktop-presets|The New bot dialog with the Security preset picked, showing Tech, Non-tech and Lifestyle tabs and the avatar grid.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Personas and souls

Start from a preset. Every bot has a persona for what it does, a soul for how it speaks, and a pixel avatar.

  • Handoffs with an @mention

    Mention a teammate, or @everyone, and they answer next in the same chat, up to four handoffs per message.

  • Team discussions

    Ask a team and the lead runs a short discussion, then posts one summary. Teammates can pass when they have nothing to add.

  • Channels and direct chats

    Talk to the whole team in a channel or to one bot directly. Threads keep side topics apart.

  • The Laya router

    Picks which bot answers in a channel, and the model and effort for bots set to auto. If it is offline, kwant routes by keyword.

  • A receipt for every tool

    Replies list the tools a bot used and how each call ended.

  • Works on a phone

    The web app fits a phone screen, with an inbox tab for what needs you.

Spaces & accounts

Separate spaces, every account

Keep work, side projects and home apart, and use all the accounts you pay for.

{{shot:v2-space-settings|Space settings for a space, General tab: name, icon, colour, the approval rule with Ask before running commands, Ask only when risky and Don't ask, and Archive space.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Rules per space

Each space has its own approval rule and hiring rule.

{{shot:v2-accounts|Space settings, Accounts tab. The Work login is the default, in use and ready. Personal Max is out of usage and back in 3 hours. This machine needs a sign-in.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Accounts per space

See every account at a glance: which is the default, which is in use, and which ran out and when it's back.

  • Spaces

    Each space has its own bots, teams, channels, routines, skills, tool servers and accounts.

  • Quick switching

    Switch from the rail or with Ctrl/⌘ + 1–9. Every space has its own address.

  • Archive, not delete

    Archiving a space pauses its routines and keeps everything. Restore it from Archived spaces.

  • Several accounts per engine

    Claude, Codex and Grok CLI logins, Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI keys, with a default and an order.

  • Failover

    When an account runs out of usage or is signed out, the turn moves to the next one, up to three tries, and the chat says so.

  • Your own server

    Add any server that speaks the OpenAI chat or Anthropic Messages API, such as OpenRouter or LM Studio.

  • Sign in from the app

    Sign in a Claude, Codex or Grok CLI account from kwant itself.

  • Keys stay hidden

    Keys are write-only. kwant shows whether one is set, never the key.

Works on its own

kwant keeps going when you close the tab

Scheduled work, check-ins, and a list of everything still open.

Routines

Once, daily, weekly, monthly, every few minutes or hours, or a cron rule. Run now, history and pause for each.

{{shot:v2-loose-ends|Loose ends with Open, Waiting and Snoozed tabs. The top case is a letter from a water company, with the quote it's based on and a draft reply.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Loose ends

Finds questions, promises and replies that went quiet in your chats, email and GitHub.

  • Follow-ups

    A bot can set itself a one-off check-in, like looking at a deploy in an hour.

  • While you were away

    What is waiting on you, what finished and what failed or got stuck, with questions and approvals answered in place.

  • Letters

    Photograph a bill or notice. kwant reads the sender, the ask, the deadline and amounts, and reminds you before it is due.

  • Never sends

    Loose ends and letters draft the reply. You copy it or open it in your mail app.

  • Parallel Universe

    Load-test up to three ways to deploy in sealed containers on your server, next to dated price estimates. No cloud account and no spending.

  • Notifications

    Browser notifications, or an HTTPS webhook such as ntfy for your phone.

Safety & control

Autonomy you control

Bots get on with ordinary work. You choose which steps ask first, and every change can be undone.

{{shot:v2-hire-card|TechLead proposes hiring a Support bot, with the reason, what it is based on, its model and tools, and Deny, Accept once and Always accept buttons.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Hiring you approve

Bots can propose a new teammate. Accept once, always, or deny. Each space sets its rule.

{{shot:v2-passwords-bitwarden|The warning shown when turning off approvals for every bot in a space, offering to move 3 saved passwords to Bitwarden first or keep asking.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Saved passwords

Website logins in kwant's encrypted vault or your own Bitwarden. Turning approvals off requires Bitwarden.

  • Approval cards

    Allow once, Always allow or Deny, with the exact command or arguments.

  • Three approval modes

    Ask only when risky, the default for new spaces. Or ask before running commands, or don't ask. Per space, and per bot.

  • Changes with an Undo

    Bots create routines, channels and bots when asked, each with an Undo. Deletions ask first.

  • Things bots can't change

    Keys and secrets, sign-ins, approval rules and computer mode stay yours.

  • kwant's secrets are off limits

    In every mode, tool calls that name kwant's own secret files are refused.

  • Safe imports

    Imported bots arrive with no tools and no keys. Their skills wait for approval and their routines start paused.

Memory

Skills

Written procedures a bot proposes and you approve. Turn each on or off.

  • Search

    Ctrl K or ⌘K searches every channel and direct chat in the open space.

  • Bring your bots

    Import from Grok, ChatGPT, Claude or a file such as a Claude Code subagent or a SKILL.md.

  • Export

    Export a space or one bot as a file, without chat history, files, tools or keys.

  • Write your own skill

    Add a skill to a bot yourself, from its Skills tab.

Running it

One binary, on your laptop or a server

No cloud service to sign up for.

{{shot:v2-computer-mode|App settings: the Laya router status and the choice of computer for browsing and commands, Local computer or VM.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Local or VM

Bots browse and run commands on your computer, or in Docker containers kept away from your files.

{{shot:desktop-usage|A bot's Usage tab showing calls, cache share, fresh, cached and output tokens, a breakdown by purpose and recent calls.|(max-width: 720px) 100vw, (max-width: 1024px) 50vw, 570px}}

Usage

For API-key and custom-API bots, a Usage tab breaks tokens down by purpose, cache and call.

  • One binary

    kwnt has the web app and an SQLite database built in. No Node.js at runtime.

  • Laptop or VPS

    Nothing to sign in to locally. On a server, open the link it prints once. Docker Compose and a systemd unit are included.

  • Any CLI or API

    The claude, codex or grok CLI, or Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI keys.

  • MCP tools

    Add any MCP server and pick which bots can use it. OAuth for servers that need a sign-in.

  • Composio, optional

    Under Connections, add Composio to reach Gmail, Slack, Notion, Linear and more. It's off until you switch it on, and those sign-ins live on Composio's servers, not in kwant's vault.

  • A shared browser

    Bots browse in kwant's own browser profile, or in a container in VM mode.

  • Your data, one folder

    Chats, bots and settings live in ~/.kwnt by default.
