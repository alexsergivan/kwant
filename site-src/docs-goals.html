Goals: what should stay true

A goal is something that should stay true: the site answers, the nightly backup ran, the sign-up page loads. kwant checks it on a schedule, without a bot. While everything is fine you see one quiet line. When a goal breaks, a bot looks into it and you get one decision with what it found and the fix it proposes.

Make a goal

Goals are new in 0.6.5. Open the Mission board and pick New goal. Say what should stay true in a sentence, and the bot you pick drafts the checks. You review the draft before anything runs. Prefer to write them yourself? Pick Add checks myself.

You can also make a goal from finished work: on a verified task whose checks passed, Keep this true turns those checks into a goal.

A bot never starts a goal by itself. It drafts one only when you ask, a draft does nothing until you approve it, at most 3 drafts wait per space, and a draft you don't approve is removed after a week.

Checks

Checks run on their own, without a bot or a model. A goal is OK when every required check passes.

CheckWhat it looks at
A pageHTTP status, response time, whether the page shows some text, or a value in its JSON
A commandIts exit code, whether its output shows some text, or a number in its output
A fileThat it exists, that it contains some text, or that its checksum matches
  • Plain matching. "Shows" looks for the exact text you give. There are no patterns to get wrong.
  • Required or advisory. An advisory check shows on the card but doesn't decide OK.
  • How often. Every 15 minutes to once a day, every hour unless you change it. A goal counts as broken after 2 failed checks in a row by default.
  • Can't check is not Failing. When a check can't run at all, the goal says Can't check, not Failing.

When it breaks

Each goal says what happens when it breaks:

SettingWhat happens
Just tell meYou hear about it. Nothing else runs.
Suggest a fixThe default. A bot looks into it and plans a fix, which waits for you.
Fix itThe bot looks into it and fixes it, and the goal's checks confirm the fix.

A broken goal gets one Restore mission on the board. First the bot looks into it, without changing anything. With Suggest a fix, you then get one decision with the likely cause and the proposed fix, marked as the bot's report, not instructions:

  • Fix it runs the fix now.
  • Not now keeps the fix waiting. It's dropped if the goal recovers on its own.
  • Always fix this goal fixes it now, and next time without asking.

Limits that keep it calm

  • One line while everything is OK, for example "3 goals OK · checked 4 min ago".
  • At most 3 notices per goal a day, and only when its state changes.
  • Goals in the same folder that break within 10 minutes share one Restore mission, and one fix runs per folder at a time.
  • At most 2 fix attempts per break and 3 fixes a day per goal, with an optional token limit for fixes. Past that, the goal says Needs you.
  • After a fix, the folder's other goals are checked again. If one of them broke, that comes to you.
  • A fix runs with the bot's usual approvals and Flight School tier. A goal never gives a bot more freedom.
Nothing to switch on. Goals show with the Mission board. Upgrading to 0.6.5 starts no checks and no background work: nothing runs until you create or approve a goal.
