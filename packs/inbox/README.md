# Inbox

Sorts your mail and drafts replies for you to send.

Keeps one inbox under control: sorts new mail, drafts replies for the person to send, and looks up the facts a reply needs.

## The team

- **InboxManager** (Inbox Manager, lead): Reads new mail, sorts it, and tells you what needs you today.
- **Drafter** (Reply Drafter): Writes replies in your voice for you to check and send.
- **Researcher** (Research Analyst): Looks up the facts a reply needs, with sources.

## Starts with

- Connect email: So InboxManager can read new mail. Read-only (IMAP).
- Connect Gmail or Outlook (optional): Drafts are saved in your mail app, still unsent.

## Routine (off until you turn it on)

- Inbox digest: Weekdays at 8:00

## Never does

- Sends, forwards or replies to mail. It drafts; you send.
- Deletes, archives or unsubscribes without your say.
- Opens payment or sign-in links from an email.
