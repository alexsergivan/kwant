# Helpers

Everyday help: plans, looks things up and writes.

Everyday help for one person: turns loose asks into a short plan, looks things up and checks the facts, and drafts emails, posts and documents for the person to use.

## The team

- **Assistant** (Personal Assistant, lead): Plans what to do and hands out the work.
- **Researcher** (Research Analyst): Looks things up and checks the facts.
- **Writer** (Writer & Editor): Drafts emails, posts and documents.
