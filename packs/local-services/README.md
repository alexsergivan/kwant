# Local services

Answers enquiries, keeps your bookings and drafts review replies for your OK.

A ready-made front desk for a local service business: a plumber, an electrician, a cleaner, a dog groomer, a tutor. It reads enquiries and booking requests in your inbox, keeps your booking list, drafts a reminder the day before each job, and drafts replies to new reviews. Nothing goes to a customer until you have checked it and sent it yourself.

## The team

- **FrontDesk** (Front Desk, lead): answers enquiries and booking requests from your inbox, as drafts for your OK.
- **Bookings** (Bookings Assistant): keeps the list of booked jobs and drafts a reminder the day before each one.
- **Reviews** (Review Replies): drafts replies to new reviews for you to check and post. Unhappy reviews come to you instead.

## It asks you

- What is your business called? (for example, Bright Spark Electrical)
- What do you offer? (for example, rewiring, fuse boxes and EV chargers)
- Where do you work? (for example, North Leeds and nearby villages)

You can skip these. The team then asks you once in its chat.

## Starts with

- Connect email: so FrontDesk can read enquiries, booking requests and review alerts. Read-only (IMAP). Done when an IMAP source in Loose ends checks OK.
- Connect Gmail or Outlook (optional): drafts are saved in your mail app, still unsent. Done when a Gmail or Outlook app connection is active.
- Connect your calendar (optional): so Bookings can see free times and the jobs booked for tomorrow. Done when a Google Calendar or Outlook calendar connection is active.

## Routines (off until you turn them on)

- Morning enquiries: weekdays at 8:00, FrontDesk drafts replies to new enquiries.
- New reviews: weekdays at 10:00, Reviews drafts replies to new reviews.
- Booking reminders: every day at 17:00, Bookings drafts reminders for tomorrow's jobs.

## Never does

- Changes prices or quotes one that isn't on your price list.
- Offers refunds, discounts or anything free.
- Replies to an angry or upset customer. Those come to you.
- Sends anything to a customer without your OK. It drafts; you send.

It also never adds tools, connects apps or changes approvals. You set those up yourself.
