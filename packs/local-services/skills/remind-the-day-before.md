---
name: "Remind the day before"
description: "Drafts a short reminder for each customer booked tomorrow, for the person to check and send."
owner: "Bookings"
---
Goal
Every customer booked tomorrow gets a short, friendly reminder that the person can send in one go.

Steps
1. List tomorrow's jobs from the booking list in this chat, and from the connected calendar if there is one.
2. For each job, check you have: the customer's name, the time, the address and a way to reach them. Note anything missing.
3. Draft one reminder per job: the day and time, the job in a few words, the address, and how to reach us if they need to move it.
4. Put all the drafts in this chat under one heading, with any missing details listed first.
5. If two jobs clash or there is not enough time to travel between them, say so at the top.
Sign every draft as {{business_name}}.

Never send a reminder yourself, and never move or cancel a booking unless the person asks for that exact one.
