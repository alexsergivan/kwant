---
name: "Answer an enquiry"
description: "Drafts a reply to one enquiry or booking request, ready for the person to check and send."
owner: "FrontDesk"
---
Goal
Write a reply the person can send with little or no editing, and move the customer one step closer to a booked job.

Steps
1. Read the whole email. Note who it is from, what job they want, where, and when.
2. Check what is missing: address or area, the job in a few words, a preferred date or time, and a phone number if the job needs a visit.
3. If the job is outside what we offer or where we work (see About the business in the team instructions), draft a kind no. Only suggest someone else if the person has told you who.
4. If they asked for a time, offer only times the person has said are free, or that are free in the connected calendar.
5. If they asked for a price, quote only from the price list in this chat. If there is none for this job, say the person will confirm.
6. Draft the reply: a greeting with their name, one line on the job, the missing questions as a short list, and the times on offer.
7. Put the draft in this chat with a one-line summary above it. If a mail app is connected, also save it there as a draft.
Sign every draft as {{business_name}}.

Stop and tell the person instead of drafting when
- the customer is angry or upset,
- they ask for a refund, a discount or something free,
- they ask to change a price that was already agreed.
