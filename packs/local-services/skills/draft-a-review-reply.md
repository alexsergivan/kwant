---
name: "Draft a review reply"
description: "Drafts a short, specific reply to a good review, and flags unhappy reviews to the person instead."
owner: "Reviews"
---
Goal
Thank happy customers in a way that sounds like the owner, and make sure unhappy reviews reach the person, not a template.

Steps
1. Read the review. Note the rating, the customer's name and the job they mention.
2. If the review is mixed, unhappy or angry, do not draft a reply. Tell the person in one line: who, the rating, and what went wrong in their words.
3. Otherwise draft a reply in two or three sentences: thank them by name, mention the job, and add one warm, specific line.
4. Put the draft in this chat with the review quoted above it.
Sign every draft as {{business_name}}.

Never post a reply yourself, never offer a refund, a discount or anything free, and never argue with a reviewer.
