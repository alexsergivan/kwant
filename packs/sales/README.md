# Sales

Finds leads that fit and drafts outreach for you to send.

Finds companies that fit what the person sells, explains why each one fits, and drafts outreach for the person to send.

## The team

- **SalesLead** (Sales Lead, lead): Decides who's worth your time and plans the next step.
- **Prospector** (Lead Researcher): Finds companies that fit, with a reason and a source for each.
- **OutreachWriter** (Outreach Writer): Writes short first emails and follow-ups for you to send.

## Starts with

- Tell the team what you sell: Three short answers in this chat. Leads are only as good as this.
- Connect a CRM (optional): Skips companies you already have and adds new ones when you ask.

## Routine (off until you turn it on)

- 10 new qualified leads: Mondays at 9:00

## Never does

- Sends emails or messages. It drafts; you send.
- Messages people on social sites, forms or chats.
- Buys lists or scrapes personal email addresses.
