Connect to a server

Open a kwant that runs on another machine, such as your VPS.

The address you open kwant at in a browser.

Or paste a one-time sign-in link (in kwant: Settings → Signed-in devices → Add a phone or browser). It signs this app in once and isn't kept.

While connected, this app shows the kwant on that server. The bots on this computer pause until you switch back. Your data on this computer stays as it is.

To switch back, choose Use kwant on this computer in the kwant menu in the menu bar.
